# RockSpin: rock the molecule to show its depth

In a still image of a molecule, it is hard to tell what is in front and what is behind.
Rocking the view a little makes the depth much easier to read.
**RockSpin** creates this motion with a single animation element. Motions like PyMOL's and ChimeraX's
rock / nutate / wobble play back as soon as you add the element from the Animation panel.

## Three kinds of motion

| Pattern | Motion |
|---|---|
| **Rock** | Swings back and forth around an axis (default) |
| **Nutate** | The line of sight moves around a circle |
| **Wobble** | Swings in a figure-eight |

## Try it

1. Display a molecule and orient it the way you want to show it.
2. Open the **Animation** tab of the bottom panel, and choose **Rock** from **Add element** (+) in the left column.
3. Click **Play** in the header. By default, the view swings 15° to each side around the Y axis (vertical on the screen), one round trip in 1 second.
4. Select the strip to show its settings in the inspector on the right. **Pattern** changes the kind of motion,
   **Swing angle** the amplitude, and **Cycles** the number of repetitions.
   To rock more slowly, lengthen the element with **Duration**.
5. Turn **Loop** in the header ON to play repeatedly. When Cycles is a whole number, the view returns to where it started at the end of the element,
   so the rocking continues without a seam.

The same RockSpin element can also be added with `rock` / `wobble` in ChX console,
or with `movie.rock` / `movie.nutate` and similar commands in PyM console.
Export it as a movie, and it is ready for presentation slides or social media.
